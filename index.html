⑟.Waste Battery Management

1Description of Hazardous Substances

This battery does not contain mercury, cadmium or lead exceeding the regulatory limits. The lithium-ion electrolyte contained may cause fire when damaged. Do not puncture or throw it into the fire.

2Correct classification and placement guidelines

Do not discard in household garbage or mixed garbage

Please place the used batteries in the designated battery recycling bins or return them to the retailer's recycling points.

3Environmental and Health Impacts of Improper Disposal

  • Heavy metals (e.g., lead, cadmium, mercury) may leak into soil and groundwater, contaminating water sources and ecosystems
  • Damaged or overheated lithium batteries may catch fire, causing air pollution and fire hazards
  • Hazardous substances enter the human body through the food chain, potentially damaging kidneys and the nervous system over time
  • Toxic fumes from incineration can irritate the respiratory tract and harm health
